Buzz: free offline transcription with Whisper

Buzz is an open-source desktop app that turns speech in audio or video files into text. It uses OpenAI's Whisper models and runs entirely on your computer, so recordings do not leave your device.

Who it suits

  • Journalists and researchers with private recordings
  • Creators who want a caption file without uploading videos
  • Anyone who wants transcripts for meetings and interviews

Getting started

  1. Download Buzz from its project page and install it.
  2. Open the app and choose a Whisper model size. Smaller is faster, larger is more accurate.
  3. Add your audio or video file and start the transcription.
  4. Wait for it to finish, then review the text.
  5. Export as SRT, VTT or plain text.

What you get for free

Buzz is free and open source. Models are downloaded the first time you use them.

Things to watch

  • Large models are slow without a good graphics card.
  • Accuracy drops with background noise and heavy accents.
  • Proofread before publishing.

The short version

A private, free way to get a transcript or caption file. Choose a model that matches your computer.
