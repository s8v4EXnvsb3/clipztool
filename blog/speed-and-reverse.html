How to slow down, speed up or reverse a video

Changing the speed of a clip is one of the quickest ways to make footage more interesting. The Clipztool speed tool slows a video down, speeds it up or plays it backwards.

Who it suits

  • Creators adding slow motion to a highlight
  • People speeding up a long screen recording
  • Anyone who wants a quick rewind effect

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Speed and reverse.
  2. Add your video.
  3. Pick an effect: 0.5x, 0.75x, 1.5x, 2x, 4x or Reverse.
  4. Press Run.
  5. Check the preview, then download it.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. Processing happens in your browser.

Things to watch

  • Reverse works on clips up to 30 seconds. Trim longer videos first.
  • Sound is sped up or slowed down along with the picture, so voices change pitch less than you might expect but still sound different.
  • Slow motion on footage shot at a normal frame rate can look choppy, because there are no extra frames to show.

The short version

Great for short effects. For smooth slow motion, film at a higher frame rate in the first place.
