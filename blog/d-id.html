D-ID: animate a photo so it speaks your script

D-ID animates a still photo so the person in it appears to speak. You give it an image and a script, and it generates a short talking video. It is often used for explainers, greetings and simple presenter clips.

Who it suits

  • Teams who want a presenter without filming
  • Educators making short spoken lessons
  • People experimenting with talking-photo effects

Getting started

  1. Sign up for a free trial on the D-ID site.
  2. Upload a clear, front-facing photo or pick a provided presenter.
  3. Paste your script or upload audio.
  4. Generate the video.
  5. Review it, then download.

What you get for free

D-ID has offered a free trial with limited credits, and trial videos may be watermarked. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Only use photos you have permission to animate. Never animate someone else's face to mislead.
  • Photos with a clear face, even lighting and a neutral expression give the best results.
  • A free trial is short, so test your idea quickly.

The short version

Easy to try, with strong ethical lines to respect. Use your own photo or a licensed presenter.
