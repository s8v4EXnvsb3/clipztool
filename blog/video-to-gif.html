How to turn a video into a GIF for free

A GIF is still the easiest way to show a short moment in a chat, a document or a support ticket. The Clipztool converter takes a few seconds of any video and turns them into an animated GIF without uploading the file anywhere.

Who it suits

  • People making reaction clips or product demos
  • Support and documentation writers who need short screen captures
  • Anyone who wants a looping clip that plays without a video player

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Video to GIF.
  2. Add your video.
  3. Set the Start time and a Length of up to 15 seconds.
  4. Pick a width (320, 480 or 640 pixels) and a frame rate (8, 12 or 15).
  5. Press Run, check the preview, then download the GIF.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. Nothing is uploaded; the conversion happens on your device.

Things to watch

  • GIF files get large fast. A 5 second clip at 480 pixels and 12 frames per second is a good starting point.
  • The length is capped at 15 seconds. For anything longer, share the video itself.
  • GIFs have no sound, so the audio is dropped.

The short version

Keep clips short and narrow. If the file is still too big, lower the width or the frame rate before you shorten the clip.
