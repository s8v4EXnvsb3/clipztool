ComfyUI: running AI video models on your own computer

ComfyUI is an open-source app that lets you run AI image and video models on your own computer. There are no credits and no queue. The cost is setup time and a capable graphics card, and in return you control everything.

Who it suits

  • People who want unlimited generations and no account
  • Hobbyists who like tuning every step of a workflow
  • Anyone with a recent NVIDIA graphics card and some patience

Getting started

  1. Download ComfyUI from the official site and install it.
  2. Open the app and browse the built-in workflow templates.
  3. Pick a video template and let ComfyUI list the models it needs.
  4. Download those models, which are large files, and place them where the app asks.
  5. Type a prompt, press run and wait for the clip.

What you get for free

ComfyUI is free and open source. The models you load have their own licences, so read each one before using output commercially.

Things to watch

  • Video models need a lot of graphics memory. A weak card will be slow or fail.
  • Model files are large, often many gigabytes each.
  • Node-based workflows look complicated at first. Start from a template and change one thing at a time.

The short version

The best choice if you generate a lot and have the hardware. If you only want a few clips, a hosted tool is much simpler.
