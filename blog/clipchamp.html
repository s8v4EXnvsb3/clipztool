Clipchamp: a free browser video editor with auto captions

Clipchamp is a video editor from Microsoft that runs in your browser. It is simple enough for a first project and has useful extras like automatic captions and text to speech, which turns typed text into a voiceover.

Who it suits

  • People who want a no-install editor
  • Anyone who needs a quick voiceover without recording one
  • Teams making simple explainers and training clips

Getting started

  1. Open Clipchamp in your browser and sign in with a Microsoft account.
  2. Create a new video and import your clips.
  3. Drag clips onto the timeline and trim them.
  4. Add captions with the auto caption tool, or add a text to speech voiceover.
  5. Export the video.

What you get for free

The free plan supports basic editing and exporting at standard definition up to full HD, with some stock content and features reserved for paid plans. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Heavy projects can feel slow in a browser.
  • Premium stock media is marked and will not export on the free plan.
  • Always listen to a text to speech voiceover to check pronunciation.

The short version

A friendly editor for simple projects, especially if you already use a Microsoft account.
