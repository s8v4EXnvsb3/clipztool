How to merge several video clips into one

Putting clips one after another is the simplest edit there is. The Clipztool merger joins two or more videos into a single file in the order you choose.

Who it suits

  • People combining several phone recordings into one
  • Creators stitching an intro, a main clip and an outro
  • Anyone who needs one file instead of a folder of parts

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Merge clips.
  2. Add two or more videos. Use the arrows to change their order.
  3. Pick an output size: wide, vertical or square.
  4. Press Run.
  5. Preview the merged video and download it.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. Your clips stay on your device.

Things to watch

  • Every clip is scaled to the output size with black bars where needed, so mixed sizes line up.
  • If any clip has no audio, the merged video is silent. The page tells you when this happens.
  • Merging many large clips needs a lot of memory. Compress or trim them first if the browser struggles.

The short version

Best for a handful of short clips. For crossfades, titles and audio mixing, use a full editor.
