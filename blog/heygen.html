HeyGen: making presenter videos from a script

HeyGen produces videos where a digital presenter speaks your script. You type the words, choose an avatar and a voice, and it generates the video. It suits explainers and training where filming a person would be slow or costly.

Who it suits

  • Teams making training or onboarding videos
  • Small businesses producing product explainers
  • Creators who do not want to appear on camera

Getting started

  1. Create a free account on the HeyGen site.
  2. Start a new video and choose an avatar.
  3. Paste your script and choose a voice and language.
  4. Generate the video and preview it.
  5. Download it or share the link.

What you get for free

The free plan is limited in video length and number of videos, and output may carry a watermark. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Keep scripts short and conversational.
  • Always tell viewers when a presenter is AI generated if it could mislead them.
  • Check the rules on using avatars based on real people.

The short version

A quick way to produce a presenter-led video, best for short and clear information.
