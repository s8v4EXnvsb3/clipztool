Hailuo AI: free text and image to video, explained

Hailuo AI generates short videos from a text prompt or a starting image. People like it for smooth motion and for how closely it follows a prompt that describes a scene and a camera move.

Who it suits

  • Creators testing several AI video tools side by side
  • People who want cinematic camera moves in a short clip
  • Anyone building a storyboard before filming

Getting started

  1. Sign up on the Hailuo AI site.
  2. Choose text to video or image to video.
  3. Describe the subject, the setting and the camera movement in one or two sentences.
  4. Generate and watch the result.
  5. Adjust the wording and run it again if the motion is off.

What you get for free

Free accounts have had a credit allowance for generating clips, with limits that can change. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Generation times can be longer when the service is busy.
  • Be careful with prompts that include real people or branded content.
  • Always check the licence before using clips commercially.

The short version
