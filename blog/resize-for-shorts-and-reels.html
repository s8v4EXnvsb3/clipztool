How to resize a video for Shorts, Reels and TikTok

Each platform likes a different shape. Shorts, Reels and TikTok want vertical video. Instagram feed posts like 4:5. YouTube wants wide. The Clipztool resizer changes the shape of a clip in your browser.

Who it suits

  • Creators reposting one clip to several platforms
  • Anyone who recorded sideways and needs a vertical version
  • Teams making square versions for ads or newsletters

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Resize for Shorts and Reels.
  2. Add your video.
  3. Pick a shape: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9.
  4. Pick how to fit: fill the frame, which crops the edges, or show everything, which adds black bars.
  5. Press Run, preview it, then download.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. The video never leaves your device.

Things to watch

  • Filling the frame crops the sides. Check that faces and text stay inside the picture.
  • Output sizes are 720 by 1280 for vertical, 720 by 900 for 4:5, 720 by 720 for square and 1280 by 720 for wide. They are fine for social posts, not for large screens.
  • Upscaling a small video to a larger frame will not add detail.

The short version

Use fill for talking-head clips and show-everything for screen recordings, where cropping would cut off the interface.
