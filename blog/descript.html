Descript: edit video by editing the transcript

Descript works differently from other editors. It transcribes your video, then you edit the text. Delete a sentence and the matching part of the video is cut. For talking-head videos, interviews and podcasts, that is much faster than scrubbing a timeline.

Who it suits

  • Podcasters and interviewers
  • People who record talks, webinars or tutorials
  • Anyone who finds a traditional timeline slow

Getting started

  1. Create a free Descript account and a new project.
  2. Import your video or audio and let it transcribe.
  3. Read through the transcript and delete mistakes, filler words and tangents.
  4. Add captions or a title if needed.
  5. Export the video or just the audio.

What you get for free

The free plan has included a limited amount of transcription and some export restrictions. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Transcripts are only as good as the audio. Clean recordings give better results.
  • Text-based cuts can leave jumpy edits, so watch the video back.
  • Heavy visual editing is easier in a traditional editor.

The short version

A great fit for spoken-word video. Less useful for footage with little talking.
