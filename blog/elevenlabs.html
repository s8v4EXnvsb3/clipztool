ElevenLabs: free text to speech for video voiceovers

ElevenLabs turns written text into natural-sounding speech. For video makers, that means a voiceover without a microphone, in many voices and languages. It also offers dubbing, which translates speech from one language to another.

Who it suits

  • Creators making narrated videos without recording audio
  • Teams producing training and explainer videos
  • Anyone adding a voice to a slide or screen recording

Getting started

  1. Create a free account on the ElevenLabs site.
  2. Open the text to speech tool and paste your script.
  3. Choose a voice and listen to a short sample.
  4. Generate the audio and download it.
  5. Add the audio file to your video in an editor.

What you get for free

Free plans have a monthly limit on how much text you can convert, and free use may come with conditions on credit and commercial use. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Listen for odd pronunciation, especially for names and acronyms. Respell them if needed.
  • Do not clone or imitate a real person's voice without permission.
  • The monthly allowance is small compared with long videos.

The short version

Excellent quality for short voiceovers. Plan the script before you spend your monthly allowance.
