Video2X: free AI upscaling for low-resolution video

Video2X is open-source software that makes low-resolution video bigger and smoother using AI models. It can upscale old footage to a higher resolution and increase the frame rate. It runs on your own computer.

Who it suits

  • People restoring old home videos or low-resolution clips
  • Anime and animation fans upscaling older shows
  • Hobbyists comfortable running software from a project page

Getting started

  1. Download Video2X from its project page and read the setup notes.
  2. Check that your graphics card supports the technology it uses.
  3. Choose an input video and an upscaling model.
  4. Pick the output size, for example double the original.
  5. Run it and wait. It can take a long time.

What you get for free

Video2X is free and open source. The models it uses have their own terms, so check them if you plan to share or sell the result.

Things to watch

  • It needs a reasonably modern graphics card, and big videos take hours.
  • Upscaling guesses at detail. It improves sharpness but cannot recover what was never recorded.
  • Setup is more technical than a website tool.

The short version

A solid free option for restoration projects if you do not mind the setup.
