CapCut for beginners: editing and auto captions for free

CapCut is a video editor built around short, social-style videos. It runs on phones, desktop and in the browser, and it is popular for its automatic captions, ready-made effects and quick background removal.

Who it suits

  • Creators making TikTok, Reels and Shorts
  • Anyone who wants captions generated for them
  • Beginners who want templates and effects without a steep learning curve

Getting started

  1. Install CapCut or open it in your browser and sign in.
  2. Create a project and import your video.
  3. Use the captions feature to generate text from the speech, then fix any mistakes.
  4. Add effects, music or a background change if you want them.
  5. Export at the quality you need.

What you get for free

The free plan covers the main editing features. Some effects, templates and exports sit behind a paid plan. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Always proofread automatic captions. Names and unusual words are often wrong.
  • Some effects and templates are marked as Pro and will not export on a free account.
  • Check the terms on how your videos and data are used.

The short version

A fast route from raw footage to a captioned short video.
