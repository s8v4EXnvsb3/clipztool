Runway: what you can do with the free credits

Runway is a larger toolbox than most AI video sites. It generates video from text and images, and it also has editing tools such as background removal and object removal. That makes it useful for both making and fixing footage.

Who it suits

  • Creators who want generation and editing in one place
  • Editors who need to remove a background or an unwanted object
  • Teams exploring AI video for concept work

Getting started

  1. Sign up for a free account on the Runway site.
  2. Open a video generation tool and pick text or image as the starting point.
  3. Write a short prompt or upload a reference image.
  4. Generate, then review the result.
  5. Try an editing tool, such as green screen, on your own footage.

What you get for free

Free plans typically come with a small, limited pool of credits rather than a monthly refill, and some features and exports are restricted. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Credits run out quickly, especially on longer or higher quality generations.
  • Some of the best tools sit behind paid plans.
  • Read the licence terms before using output in client or commercial work.

The short version

Worth an account for the editing tools alone. Use the credits on the things only Runway does well.
