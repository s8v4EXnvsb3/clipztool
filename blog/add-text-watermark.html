How to add a text watermark to a video

A small name or handle in the corner tells viewers where a clip came from, even if it gets reposted. The Clipztool watermark tool burns a line of text onto a video for its whole length.

Who it suits

  • Creators protecting clips from being passed off as someone else's
  • Brands adding a handle to short promos
  • Teachers marking draft or internal videos

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Add text watermark.
  2. Add your video.
  3. Type your text, for example @yourname.
  4. Pick a position and a size.
  5. Press Run, preview it, then download.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. No upload is involved.

Things to watch

  • The text is part of the picture once exported. Keep your original file in case you need a clean copy.
  • A mark in the corner can be cropped out. A mark nearer the middle is harder to remove but more distracting.
  • Medium size is readable on phones without covering the action.

The short version

Pick a corner, keep it small and keep the original. That covers most uses.
