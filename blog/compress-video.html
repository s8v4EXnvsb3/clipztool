How to compress a video without ruining it

A phone recording can easily run to several hundred megabytes. That is too large for most email services and slow to upload anywhere. The Clipztool compressor re-encodes a video at a smaller size and keeps it playable everywhere.

Who it suits

  • People who hit an upload or email size limit
  • Anyone sharing a clip over a slow connection
  • Creators who want a lighter copy to review or send for feedback

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Compress video.
  2. Add your video.
  3. Choose Compression: Light, Balanced or Strong.
  4. Choose a Maximum width, or keep the original size.
  5. Press Run and compare the result size with the original shown under the preview.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. Nothing is uploaded.

Things to watch

  • Balanced at a maximum width of 1280 pixels works for most clips. Strong can look blocky on fast motion.
  • Compressing an already small file can make it larger. The page shows both sizes so you can check.
  • Each pass loses a little quality. Compress from your original, not from a copy you already compressed.

The short version

Start with Balanced. If the result is still too big, lower the maximum width before you choose Strong.
