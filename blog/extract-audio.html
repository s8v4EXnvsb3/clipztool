How to extract audio from a video as an MP3

Sometimes you only need the sound: a lecture to listen to on a walk, a voice memo recorded on video, music from your own footage. The Clipztool audio extractor saves the audio track of a video as an MP3.

Who it suits

  • Students and researchers working from recorded talks
  • Podcasters who record on video first
  • Editors who need a clean audio file for another tool

Getting started

  1. Open the editor and choose Extract audio.
  2. Add your video.
  3. Pick a quality: High, Medium or Small file.
  4. Press Run.
  5. Play the MP3 in the page and download it.

What you get for free

Built in and free with a Clipztool account. The video stays on your device.

Things to watch

  • If the video has no audio track, the tool tells you instead of producing an empty file.
  • MP3 is compressed. If you need a lossless master, use a desktop editor.
  • Only extract audio from recordings you have the right to use.

The short version

A quick way to turn a video into something you can listen to. Choose High for speech you will edit later and Small for casual listening.
