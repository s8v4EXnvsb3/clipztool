Kling AI: a guide to free video generation

Kling AI creates short videos from text prompts and images. It is one of the main alternatives to Luma and Runway, and it is a good tool to try if you want realistic motion in a short shot.

Who it suits

  • Creators comparing AI video generators
  • People animating product photos or portraits
  • Anyone who wants to try AI video before paying for it

Getting started

  1. Sign up on the Kling AI site.
  2. Choose text to video or image to video.
  3. Write what happens in the shot and how the camera moves.
  4. Generate the clip and review it.
  5. Download your favourite or refine the prompt.

What you get for free

Kling has offered free credits on its free plan. How often they refill and what they cover can change. Check the current terms. Plans and limits change often, so read the current pricing page before you plan a project around it.

Things to watch

  • Free generations can queue for a while at busy times.
  • Start from a clear, well lit image if you animate a photo.
  • Review the terms before using results in paid work.

The short version

A strong option to include in your tests. Use the same prompt on two or three tools and keep the best clip.
