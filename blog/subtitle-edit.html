Subtitle Edit: free subtitles with built-in speech recognition

Subtitle Edit is a free, open-source subtitle editor. It can create subtitles from scratch, fix timing, convert between formats and generate a first draft from speech using Whisper speech recognition.

Who it suits

  • Anyone adding subtitles to a video before upload
  • Translators who work with timed text
  • People who need to convert subtitle formats

Getting started

  1. Download Subtitle Edit from the official site and install it.
  2. Open your video from the File menu so you can watch while you edit.
  3. Use the speech recognition option to generate a first draft.
  4. Correct the text and adjust timing where lines start or end too early or late.
  5. Save as SRT or another format and upload it with your video.

What you get for free

Subtitle Edit is free and open source.

Things to watch

  • Speech recognition models are downloaded separately, and larger ones need a good computer.
  • Always proofread the draft, especially names and technical terms.
  • Check which subtitle format your target platform accepts.

The short version

The best free tool for polishing subtitles on a computer, even if you use another tool for the first draft.
