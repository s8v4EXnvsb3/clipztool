    How free is free

    Free almost always comes with a catch. These are the five kinds you will run into, so you know what to expect before you sign up.

    Free plan
    You can use it as long as you like, with caps. Common limits are watermarks, lower resolution, shorter exports and fewer monthly minutes.
    Credits
    You get a pool of credits, often refilled daily or monthly. A short generated clip uses several, so expect a handful of clips, not hours.
    Trial
    Free for a limited time or a fixed amount. Good for testing whether the tool fits, not for a long project.
    Built in
    Clipztool's own editors. They run on your device, cost nothing and need no account. Your video is never uploaded.
    Open source
    No account and no limits, but it runs on your own computer. Video models want a recent graphics card.

    Plans, prices and limits change often. Check each site before you build a project around it.
