Edit video in your browser.

Pick a tool, add a video and press Run. Sign in with your email, then your video is processed on this computer and never uploaded anywhere.

Create a free account to use the editor

Enter your email and we will send you a sign-in link. There is no password, and the same link creates your account the first time.

    The first run downloads the video engine, about 25 MB, then it is cached. Long or very large videos can be slow or run out of memory, especially on phones. These tools use ffmpeg running in WebAssembly.

    Clipztool is independent and free. Your videos never leave your device. We only keep the email address you sign in with.
